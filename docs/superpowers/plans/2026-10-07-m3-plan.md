# Hisab M3 — Plan (Recurring, EMI Loans, Budgets) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Salaries, rent and subscriptions recur on their own: each one shows as due and is confirmed with one tap. Each EMI loan shows months left, amount left, next due date and "debt-free by", and is marked paid in one tap. Monthly budgets show progress and a "safe to spend per day" figure. Home gets the **Due soon** strip.

**Architecture:** Same layering as M2. The date and money rules are pure functions in `packages/core` (spec §6.3, §6.5, §6.6). SQL and mutations live in `packages/db` (tested on node:sqlite). The UI is in `apps/mobile` (Plan tab, Loan detail, forms, and the Home Due strip).

**Tech Stack:** As M2.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-hisab-design.md` §5.2 (recurring_rules, recurring_skips, loans, budgets), §6.3, §6.5, §6.6, §7.3 (Plan, Loan detail, Home Due soon)

## Global Constraints
- Month arithmetic clamps to the month end (a rule anchored on the 31st falls on Feb 28/29).
- A recurring rule posts at most one transaction per `occurrence_date`. The DB unique index enforces this; client code must also not double-post.
- Occurrence dates are local `YYYY-MM-DD`. "Due" means `occurrence_date <= today` and the occurrence is neither posted nor skipped.
- **Auto** rules post due occurrences automatically when the app opens or returns to the foreground. **Confirm** rules show in Due soon.
- EMI: `paid = installments_paid_before + count(emi tx)`. `remaining = monthsLeft × emi`. "Debt-free by" follows spec §6.3 exactly.
- Budgets are monthly; `category_id = null` is the overall budget. Safe-to-spend per day only shows when an overall budget exists.

## Review Focus
1. **Anchors on the 29th/30th/31st** in short months, and yearly anchors on Feb 29. Tested in Task 1.
2. **A rule created today with an anchor in the past** must not flood Due with old occurrences. Due starts at `max(anchor, created day)`. Tested in Task 1.
3. **A loan already partly paid before Hisab** (`installments_paid_before`) gives correct months left and next due date. Tested in Task 1.
4. **Two devices auto-posting the same occurrence offline:** the second upload gets 23505, which the connector treats as done (M1 fix). Locally, a re-run must not post twice. Tested in Task 2 (`postDueAutoRules` is idempotent).
5. **Marking an EMI paid twice quickly** (double tap) creates only one installment. Tested in Task 2 (`markEmiPaid` checks the next installment number inside one write transaction).

### Task 1: core — recurrence, loans, budgets
- `occurrences(rule: {frequency, interval, anchor_date, end_date?}, from: string, to: string): string[]`
- `dueOccurrences(rule & {created_on, paused}, posted: Set<string>, skipped: Set<string>, today: string, lookaheadDays = 7): {date, overdue: boolean}[]`
- `loanProgress(loan: {emi_amount_minor, total_installments, first_due_date, installments_paid_before}, paidCount: number, paidAmount: number, today: string)` returns `{ paid, monthsLeft, paidAmount, remainingAmount, nextDueDate | null, isOverdue, debtFreeBy | null, installmentDate(n) }`
- `budgetProgress(spent, limit)` returns `{ ratio, state: 'ok'|'near'|'over' }` (near at 80% or more), and `safeToSpendPerDay(limit, spent, today)`.

### Task 2: db — queries and mutations
- **Queries:**
  - `Q.recurringRules` (with category, account and party names)
  - `Q.postedOccurrences(ruleId)`
  - `Q.skips`
  - `Q.loansWithPayments` (paid count and sum of non-deleted EMI transactions)
  - `Q.loanPayments(loanId)`
  - `Q.budgetsWithSpent(start, end)`
- **Mutations:**
  - `createRecurringRule`, `updateRecurringRule`, `pauseRecurringRule`, `deleteRecurringRule` (soft)
  - `postOccurrence(ex, userId, rule, date, override?)` and `skipOccurrence`
  - `postDueAutoRules(ex, userId, today)`
  - `createLoan`, `updateLoan`, `closeLoan`, `markEmiPaid(ex, userId, loanId, {account_id, amount_minor?, occurred_on})`
  - `setBudget(ex, userId, categoryId | null, amount)` (upsert per category) and `removeBudget`
- Validators for rule, loan and budget inputs mirror the Postgres CHECKs.

### Task 3: mobile — Plan tab
A segmented control with three sections:
- **Budgets:** overall card with safe-to-spend per day, per-category bars (orange when over), add/edit budget sheet.
- **Recurring:** rules with next date, amount, mode badge and pause; add/edit rule screen.
- **Loans:** cards with segmented progress and "9 of 24 · 15 left", and an add-loan screen.

### Task 4: mobile — Loan detail and Home "Due soon"
- **Loan detail:**
  - Progress ring ("15 months left"), amounts paid and remaining, "Debt-free by".
  - A "Next EMI" card with ✓ Mark paid (amount editable by long press).
  - Payment history, plus close and edit actions.
- **Home "Due soon" strip:**
  - Covers confirm-mode recurring items, EMIs within 7 days, and overdue items.
  - Overdue items are marked in amber with a "● overdue" label.
  - ✓ posts the item; a long press opens the quick-log sheet prefilled.
  - Each strip item can also be skipped.
- Auto-rules are posted on app foreground.

### Task 5: verification
`turbo typecheck test`, the web build, the Android bundle, and the ledger.
