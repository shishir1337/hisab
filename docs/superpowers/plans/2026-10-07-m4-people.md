# Hisab M4 — People, Reminders & Notifications Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:**
- Money lent or borrowed is tracked per person, with partial repayments.
- Overdue lendings nag the owner. One tap opens WhatsApp or SMS with a polite, editable message.
- EMIs, recurring items, budgets and a daily "log something" nudge produce local notifications, which work offline.

**Architecture:**
- **`packages/core`:** pure lending status, reminder text and URLs, phone normalization, and the notification planner (spec §6.4, §6.8).
- **`packages/db`:** lending and repayment mutations and people queries, tested on node:sqlite.
- **`apps/mobile`:** People tab, person detail, lend and repay forms, an `expo-notifications` reconciler, and budget-threshold alerts after each save.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-hisab-design.md` §5.2 (parties, lendings, lending_reminders_sent), §6.4, §6.6 thresholds, §6.8, §7.3 People / Person detail

## Global Constraints
- **Outstanding:** for **lent**, outstanding = principal − Σ `lending_in`; for **borrowed**, outstanding = principal − Σ `lending_out`. The initial movement (an account was chosen) is never a repayment.
- **"Happened before Hisab"** creates no transaction, so no account balance changes.
- **Messages are never sent automatically.** The app only opens WhatsApp or SMS with pre-filled text, and each tap is logged in `lending_reminders_sent`.
- **Notifications** are scheduled on the device only. The 30-day window is capped at 64, with priority EMI > lending > recurring > nudge. All fire at 10:00 local, except the nudge, which fires at the user's chosen time.
- **The nudge is skipped** on any day that already has a transaction.
- **Budget alerts** fire once per category per month at 80% and at 100%.

## Review Focus
1. **Partial repayments that over-pay** (outstanding < 0) show as settled, never as a negative amount owed. Tested in Task 1.
2. **Bangladeshi local phone formats** ("01712-345678", "+880 1712 345678", "8801712345678") normalize to E.164, and garbage is rejected. Tested in Task 1.
3. **A lending with no due date** never schedules reminders, and still counts in "Owed to you". Tested in Task 1.
4. **Logging today cancels today's nudge** without touching tomorrow's. Tested in Task 1 (planner output).
5. **Deleting a repayment transaction** re-opens the lending (status is derived from transactions, never stored). Tested in Task 2.

### Task 1: core
- `lendingStatus(l, repaid, today)`
- `reminderMessage(...)`
- `whatsappUrl` and `smsUrl`
- `toE164(input, country = 'BD')`
- `planNotifications(input, now, tz)`
- `budgetThresholdsCrossed(before, after, limit)`

### Task 2: db
- **Validator:** `lendingInput`.
- **Mutations:**
  - `createLending` (with an optional initial movement)
  - `recordRepayment`, `closeLending`
  - `logReminderSent`
  - `updateParty`
- **Queries:**
  - `QL.peopleWithBalances`, `QL.lendingsForParty`, `QL.partyHistory`
  - `QL.openLendings` (for notifications and the Home hero)
  - `QL.remindersForParty`

### Task 3: mobile — People
- **People tab:** list sorted by outstanding, owed-to-you first, with search.
- **Person detail:**
  - Outstanding card with **WhatsApp · SMS · Got paid / Paid back**.
  - Editable message preview, phone editing and history.
- **Forms:** lend (new or existing person, direction, amount, dates, account or "before Hisab") and repay.
- **Quick-log "Lend" segment:** opens the lend form.
- **Home hero:** "Owed to you" uses live data.

### Task 4: mobile — Notifications
- `expo-notifications` setup: Android channel, plus a permission prompt card on Home.
- A reconciler that runs on data change (debounced) and on foreground.
- A notification tap deep-links to the loan, person, Home or quick-log.
- **Settings:** nudge toggle and time, plus the lending reminder interval. These are stored on the device in M4; server sync of profile prefs comes in M6.
- **Budget alerts:** checked after an expense is saved.

### Task 5: verification
`turbo typecheck test`, the Android bundle, the web build, and the ledger.
